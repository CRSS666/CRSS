# Glacier Anarchist Federation's 'Laws'

Welcome to the laws document for the Glacier Anarchist Federation!

We... We're anarchist, so our 'laws' are more like just suggestions
of what to and not to do so that people don't hate you.

## Do

- Keep the town organised
    - Please clean up any mess you (directly or not) make.
    A creeper exploded near you while you were walking back home?
    That sucks! But please repair your surroundings the best way
    you can.
- Replant what you chop or collect
    - Please replant any tree or crop you collect.
    This will make sure there's more crops for everybody else
    to use, and trees for people to chop and build with.
    - Of course, it's okay to chop down some trees to clear up
    an area to build something, but if you're not doing that,
    please replant them.

## Don't

- Don't *steal* anything
    - This should be obvious. Please don't steal any item from
    anywhere. We won't exactly be *mad* if you take some stuff
    without notice **and replenish it** soon enough. Odds are
    the person won't eevn notice it was gone if you replace it.
    - This doesn't excuse stealing from everyone just so you can
    get gear and then go mining for hours (or disappearing)
    to replenish the materials, you'll not have too much of a fun
    time if you get everything instantly. It's your choice if you
    want to be a lazy person, get stuff fast and abandon us because
    you got bored.
- Don't *grief* anything
    - Don't grief anything. Don't break anything without permission,
    don't *build* anything without permission.
    - If an area is clear, go ahead and build there if you want. If
    there's existing stuff, you probably shouldn't do anything there.

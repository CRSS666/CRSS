# CRSS Mods

This document contains information about
which Server-Side mods the Main CRSS Server
uses, and some recommended Client-Side mods.

If you want to suggest any addition, change
or removal of a certain mod or mod feature,
you can tell us on [our discord](https://discord.crss.cc/)
or make an issue in this repository.

The CRSS 3 branch will have a `PLUGINS.md`
file, containing similar info, but instead
applying to bukkit plugins and Babric or
Ornithe mods.

## Notes!

- No mods in this list are *required*, you'll
just have enhanced things™ if you add them.

- We will try to support as much mods as we can,
but if any of them conflict, we will pick
the most-used or most-requested option.

- If one of the mods is giving you a super-specific
'Millions to one!' issue or nitpick with one
of the mods, yell at us at discord.
